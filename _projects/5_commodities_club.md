**Brown Commodities Club**

With Stanislas Robert

An introduction to commodities markets for the club, followed by a natural gas trade pitch.

## The basics

- **What:** energy (oil, gas, power), agriculture (wheat, soybeans, softs), metals (gold, silver, copper, iron, zinc), and others like uranium, livestock, and carbon credits.
- **Who:** producers and consumers hedging, merchants and physical traders, and speculative capital (hedge funds, banks, prop shops).
- **How:** spot/physical trading and arbitrage across space, time, or form; futures; options and other derivatives.
- **Why it matters for equity investors:** commodities can be a pure signal of geopolitics, and a way to hedge equity exposures or build pair trades.

## Trade pitch: Long TTF Summer ’26 strip & Short TTF Summer ’27 strip

Thesis: the market is overpaying for European structural tightness in 2027.

- **Catalyst (i): LNG ramp-up.** US export capacity is set to more than double by 2029 on post-FID projects, and new Qatari projects add ~50% export capacity by 2027.
- **Catalyst (ii): EU policy and contracting.** Modeling European gas demand points to over-demand in 2026 and a glut in 2027. Europe must refill storage in summer 2026, projected at 25% by March, the lowest since 2021.
- **Catalyst (iii): US LNG feedgas stays abundant.** The constraint is pipes and processing, not molecules, and data centers won't crowd out LNG exports.

Why summer strips, and why a spread: summer pricing is driven more by marginal LNG cargoes than weather panic, the ’26 leg expresses a bullish refill view, and the relative-value structure doesn't need a perfect call on outright TTF levels.

Key risks: heavy European buying for summer 2027, US LNG infrastructure outages, sanctions on Russian gas, and an early end to the Russia-Ukraine war.
