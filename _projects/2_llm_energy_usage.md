**A Comparative Study of Energy Consumption for Large Language Models**

Brown University · May 2026

Analyzed statistical profiles of energy usage in LLMs, focusing on tail risks of agentic workflows.

- Dataset of 34 different models performing 5 tasks, across 838 total runs.
- Results indicated that non-reasoning and reasoning models carry statistically different distributional shapes but similar tail ratios.
